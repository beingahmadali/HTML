About the Faculty

Empowering Next-Generation Tech Leaders

The Faculty of Computing and Artificial Intelligence (FCAI) is dedicated to fostering world-class engineers, computer scientists, and researchers. Our state-of-the-art computational laboratories provide hands-on training in machine learning, software architecture, cyber security, and data science.

Admission Process Steps

Follow these sequential steps to apply for admission to FCAI:

  1. Create an account on the online University Admissions Portal.
  2. Fill in personal details and upload academic transcripts (HSSC/SSC DMCs).
  3. Select the desired degree program preferences (e.g., BSSE or BSAI).
  4. Generate and pay the processing fee voucher at an authorized bank.
  5. Appear for the FCAI Entry Test on the scheduled date.
  6. Check the Merit List published on the official website.
  7. Submit original documents and deposit semester tuition fees to confirm admission.
